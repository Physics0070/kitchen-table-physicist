A simple pendulum whose swing angle decays exponentially, giving a damped cosine motion in both horizontal and vertical coordinates

x(t) = c0 + c1*cos(c2*t + c3)*exp(-c4*t)

Fitted on the first 70% of the clip; error on the unseen rest 0.56 mm.

What it means

The bob swings like a regular pendulum, but air resistance (or internal friction) makes the swing slowly shrink. The horizontal position follows a cosine whose height drops exponentially. From the frequency we can read off g, and the decay rate tells how quickly the motion is damped.
